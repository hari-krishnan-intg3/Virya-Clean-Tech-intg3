1. Executive Summary & 5% Compounding Tariff Curve

India's electricity regulatory commissions (SERCs) increase utility grid tariffs at a compound annual growth rate of 4.8% to 6.2%. Assuming static electricity prices severely misrepresents lifetime solar yields.

Compounded Tariff(Year n) = Base Tariff × (1 + 0.05)^(n - 1)

A base commercial tariff of ₹9.50/kWh in Year 1 escalates to ₹15.47/kWh by Year 11 and ₹30.58/kWh by Year 25. A turnkey solar asset locks in generation costs at an LCOE of under ₹2.00/kWh, insulating the owner against inflation.

2. Per Capita Electricity Consumption in India

India's national benchmark per capita electricity consumption is approximately 105 kWh/month (~1,255 kWh/person/year). High-comfort homes with air conditioning and EV charging draw 180 to 250 kWh/person/month. At the Indian grid emission factor (0.82 kg CO₂/kWh), each person generates approximately 1,029 kg of operational carbon emissions yearly. Rooftop solar completely eliminates this per capita carbon footprint.

4. Solar Mat / Solar Paai (Ultralight Flexible CIGS)

Weighing only 2.9 kg/m² (vs 22 kg/m² for rigid glass panels), Solar Paai requires zero roof penetrations, bonding directly to industrial standing-seam PEB factory roofs using structural 3M adhesive film. It provides full flexibility for curved architectural roofs and solves load limit constraints on factories and warehouses.

5. Commercial & Industrial Section 32 40% AD

Commercial enterprises recover substantial capital outlays via 40% Accelerated Depreciation in Year 1. For a 100 kW installation costing ₹52,00,000, the 40% deduction yields ₹20,80,000 in depreciation write-offs, producing ₹5,40,800 in direct corporate tax savings at the standard 26% corporate tax rate.

6. Next-Generation Sodium-Ion Battery Storage (Sodium BESS)

Delivering 4,000+ deep cycles at 90% DoD, Sodium-ion chemistry functions flawlessly across a wide temperature window of -20°C to +60°C with zero risk of thermal runaway, making it the premier energy storage technology for Indian ambient heat without expensive HVAC chilling.
